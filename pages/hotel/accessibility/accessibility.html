Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to inclusive, welcoming hospitality for all.

Lumina Hospitality is committed to providing a welcoming and accessible experience for all guests. We continuously work to improve our facilities and services to ensure that everyone can enjoy the comfort and luxury of our hotel. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us in advance so we can best prepare for your stay.

accessible

Wheelchair Accessible Rooms

Wider doorways, lowered light switches, and grab bars throughout. All public areas are wheelchair accessible with ramp access.

shower

Accessible Bathrooms

Roll-in showers with fold-down benches, raised toilet seats, and lowered vanity counters for wheelchair users.

visibility

Visual Aids

Braille signage on room numbers and elevators, high-contrast wayfinding materials, and tactile floor indicators throughout common areas.

hearing

Hearing Assistance

Visual alert systems for fire alarms and door knocks, TTY phone availability, and hearing loop systems in conference rooms.

local_parking

Accessible Parking

Designated accessible parking spaces located closest to the main entrance, with level access to the lobby and elevators.

pets

Service Animals Welcome

Service animals are welcome in all areas of the hotel including restaurants, pools, and guest rooms. Water bowls available on request.
