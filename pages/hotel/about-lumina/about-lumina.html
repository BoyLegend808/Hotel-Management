Our Story

Four decades of luxury, elegance, and exceptional hospitality

A Legacy of Excellence

Since 1985, Lumina Hospitality has been synonymous with luxury, warmth, and world-class service on Victoria Island, Lagos. What began as a vision to create an extraordinary retreat has grown into one of Nigeria's most celebrated hospitality brands, welcoming guests from around the globe.

Our Journey

1985 — The Beginning

Lumina Hospitality opens its doors on Victoria Island with 80 rooms and a vision to redefine luxury in West Africa. Founded by Chief Afolabi Adesanya, the hotel quickly became a landmark destination.

1995 — Expansion

A major expansion adds 120 new rooms, the Grand Ballroom, and three additional restaurants. The hotel earns its first international recognition from the World Travel Awards.

2005 — Grand Renovation

A $50 million renovation transforms the property with modern amenities, a world-class spa, and redesigned suites. The Lumina brand becomes synonymous with Nigerian luxury hospitality.

2015 — Award-Winning

Named "Best Luxury Hotel in Africa" by the World Luxury Hotel Awards. The hotel's restaurant earns a place on Africa's 50 Best Restaurants list.

2020 — Sustainability

Launch of the Lumina Green Initiative, achieving carbon-neutral operations, installing solar panels, and partnering with local conservation programs.
