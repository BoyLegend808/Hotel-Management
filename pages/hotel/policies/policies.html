Hotel Policies

Important information about your stay with us. Please review our policies to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

login

Check-in / Check-out

Check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur additional charges. Please contact the front desk on the day of arrival for availability. Guests arriving before check-in time are welcome to use the hotel facilities while their room is being prepared.

event_busy

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled arrival time are free of charge. Cancellations made within 24 hours of arrival will be charged at 50% of the first night's rate. No-shows will be charged the full amount of the first night. During peak periods and special events, a 72-hour cancellation policy may apply.

pets

Pet Policy

We welcome well-behaved small dogs (under 15kg) in designated pet-friendly rooms. A pet fee of $50 per night applies, with a maximum of 2 pets per room. Pets must be leashed in all public areas. Please inform us at the time of booking if you wish to bring a pet. Pet amenities including beds, bowls, and treats are available upon request.

smoking_rooms

Smoking Policy

All guest rooms and indoor areas are strictly non-smoking. Designated outdoor smoking areas are available on the terrace and near the pool deck. A cleaning fee of $250 will be charged for smoking in non-smoking areas.

child_care

Children Policy

Children under 12 years old stay free when occupying the same room as their parents. Extra beds are available for $30 per night. Cribs are provided complimentary upon request. Our Kids Club offers supervised activities for children aged 4-12 daily.

credit_card

Payment Policy

We accept Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. A deposit equal to one night's stay is required at the time of booking. The remaining balance is due upon check-in. All rates are subject to applicable taxes. Currency exchange services are available at the front desk.

groups

Group Bookings

Groups booking 10 or more rooms qualify for a 10% discount on room rates. Special corporate and event rates are available. Please contact our sales team at sales@lumina.com or call +234 1 277 2701 for group inquiries.
