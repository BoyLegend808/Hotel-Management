FAQ

Everything you need to know before your stay

Check-in is available from 3:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request and are subject to availability. Please contact the front desk in advance to arrange.

Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled arrival are free of charge. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before arrival will incur a charge of 50% of the first night's rate. No-shows will be charged the full first night.

Yes, you can request a specific room type, floor, or view preference when making your reservation. While we do our best to accommodate all requests, they are subject to availability at the time of check-in.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), bank transfers, and mobile payments. Cash payments are accepted in Nigerian Naira (NGN) and US Dollars (USD).

A deposit equivalent to one night's stay is required to confirm your reservation. For group bookings and special events, a larger deposit or full prepayment may be required. The deposit is fully refundable according to our cancellation policy.

The outdoor swimming pool is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Towels are provided poolside. The pool bar operates from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Private pool cabanas can be reserved in advance.

Yes, we offer complimentary valet parking for all hotel guests. Our secure underground parking facility accommodates over 200 vehicles. Self-parking is also available in our adjacent parking structure at no additional cost.

Complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the hotel, including all rooms, restaurants, public areas, and conference facilities. Premium high-bandwidth WiFi is available for an additional fee for guests who require enhanced connectivity.

We welcome well-behaved small dogs (under 10kg) in designated pet-friendly rooms. A pet fee of ₦25,000 per night applies. Please inform us at the time of booking. Pets must be kept on a leash in all public areas. Service animals are welcome at no additional charge.

Lumina Hospitality is a non-smoking property. Smoking is prohibited in all indoor areas, including rooms and public spaces. Designated outdoor smoking areas are available near the terrace and garden. A cleaning fee of ₦50,000 will be charged for smoking in non-designated areas.
