Explore Lagos

Popular Attractions

Explore the best of Lagos, from nature reserves to world-class art galleries, all within easy reach of Lumina Hospitality.

Lekki Conservation Centre

A sprawling nature reserve featuring a canopy walkway, diverse wildlife, and tranquil mangrove forests. Perfect for nature lovers and bird watchers.

location_on 12 km away

Nike Art Gallery

One of West Africa's largest art galleries, housing an extensive collection of contemporary African art, sculptures, and traditional textiles.

location_on 8 km away

Eko Atlantic City

A modern, ambitious city built on reclaimed land featuring luxury residences, offices, retail centers, and world-class entertainment venues.

location_on 5 km away

Balogun Market

Lagos's iconic open-air marketplace offering a sensory feast of fabrics, spices, accessories, and local crafts. A true cultural experience.

location_on 15 km away

Tafawa Balewa Square

A historic landmark and ceremonial ground in Lagos Island, notable for its independence-day significance and surrounding colonial architecture.

location_on 14 km away

Ikoyi Club

An exclusive social and recreational club featuring an 18-hole golf course, tennis courts, swimming pools, and fine dining restaurants.
